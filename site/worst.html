Worst-predicted balls

The 2026 death balls XGBoost got most wrong: what happened, what each model expected, what pushed XGBoost away from the real outcome, and a hypothesis for why.

How to read this page
  • Three lists (the tabs): the ten balls with XGBoost’s highest log-loss; the ten where XGBoost lost most to the scorecard baseline (B1); and the worst three of each outcome. A ball can be in more than one list.
  • The chance line gives the probability each model put on what actually happened. Log-loss is −log of XGBoost’s chance: 1% gives 4.6, 0.1% gives 6.9.
  • The tag says whose miss it was. Rare for every model: even B1 gave it under 1%. XGBoost’s own miss: B1 gave it at least twice XGBoost’s chance. Shared miss: everything else.
  • The push bars show which groups of features moved XGBoost toward the real outcome (right) or away from it (left), starting from its guess for an average ball. They add up to the net push. Hover a bar for its multiplier.
  • The hypothesis links to one of four shared explanations below the lists. Each is written once and shared by the balls it explains.
